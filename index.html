Why does a small business need a website?

A new customer hears about you and looks you up. What they find decides whether they call, visit or move on.

  • People check before they comeHours, prices and photos are one search away.
  • You’re open when you’re closedYour site answers questions at midnight and on weekends.
  • It builds trustA clear, current site shows you’re a real business.
  • Local customers find youYou show up when people nearby search for what you do.
  • It’s yoursSocial pages can change their rules. Your site stays yours.
  • It saves you timeAnswer common questions once, not by phone every day.
